## Timezone math

- US DST ends Sun 1 Nov 2026, EU DST ends Sun 25 Oct 2026. From 1 Nov on: PST = UTC-8 and Zagreb (CET) = UTC+1, so Zagreb is PST + 9h.
- 3:00 pm PST on 4 Nov = 23:00 UTC on 4 Nov = 00:00 CET on Thu 5 Nov in Zagreb.
- Before 25 Oct both sides are on daylight time (PDT UTC-7, CEST UTC+2), offset is still +9h. Only 25 Oct to 1 Nov is odd (US on PDT, EU on CET, offset +8h).

## What the fan site "early access" claim really is

The "early access 4 to 11 Nov via invite codes distributed 20 Oct" claim is a garbled version of the official Invite-A-Friend system. Per Blizzard:

- 4 to 11 Nov is the launch week window in which people invited with a code can play without a subscription or game time. It is not early access for buyers, and it starts at launch, not before it.
- Codes: Skyborne Heroic Pack 1 code, Skyborne Epic Pack 3 codes, Warcraft Forever Collection 3 codes. Beta access is not included in the codes.
- Epic Pack and Collection also add 30 days of game time starting 4 Nov.
- Early name reservation is a perk of the paid upgrade packs, not open to everyone.

I found no official play-before-launch early access for the live game. That is an absence in what I could read, so treat it as secondary confidence only.
