# WoW Forever Planner

A temporary personal site where Filip works through every choice for playing a Horde Warlock in World of Warcraft: Forever, with a countdown to launch. Guildmates can read it.

## Language

**Decision**:
One question Filip must answer, with a set of options, a chosen option and a reason.
_Avoid_: Task, to-do, choice

**Option**:
One possible answer to a Decision, with its pros, cons and source links.
_Avoid_: Choice, alternative

**Phase**:
A period of the timeline that groups Decisions by when they are needed: Before launch, Launch prep, Leveling, Endgame.
_Avoid_: Stage, category, step

**Status**:
Where a Decision stands: open, decided or revisit.
_Avoid_: State, progress

**Deadline**:
The date by which a Decision must be made because something external closes.
_Avoid_: Due date

**Launch**:
The moment WoW Forever goes live: Wed 4 Nov 2026, 3:00 pm PST, which is 00:00 Thu 5 Nov in Zagreb.
_Avoid_: Release, go-live

**Pack**:
A paid upgrade purchase that grants perks such as name reservation.
_Avoid_: Edition, bundle
